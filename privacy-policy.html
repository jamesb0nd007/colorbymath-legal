Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Color by Math ("the App") is a children's educational coloring and math game designed for kids ages 6+. We take children's privacy extremely seriously.

Summary

We collect no personally identifiable information. The App works entirely offline for all gameplay. There are no user accounts, no behavioral analytics, no advertising, and no social features. The App does transmit a small amount of anonymous data to two service providers so it can function correctly: crash reports (Sentry) and premium subscription state (RevenueCat). Both are detailed below. No data ever contains your child's name, photos, or content they produced.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect: personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, birthdays, government IDs, location data (coarse or precise), photos or videos, audio recordings, contact lists, calendar data, browsing history, health or fitness data, or any content your child creates.

The App does transmit the following anonymous, non-identifying data to two service providers so premium purchases and crash debugging can work:

  • Anonymous device identifier — a random UUID generated by RevenueCat that identifies this installation of the App for subscription-management purposes. Not linked to your name, email, Google account, Apple ID, phone number, or any other identifier. Used only to remember which device has an active Premium subscription so we don't ask you to pay twice.
  • Purchase state — whether this installation has an active Premium subscription and which product was purchased. Sent to RevenueCat so entitlements sync across devices in a family and so we can restore purchases on reinstall.
  • Crash reports — technical information about crashes (stack trace, memory state, app version, OS version, device model) plus an anonymous installation identifier used only to group reports by device. Sent to Sentry only when a crash actually happens.
  • Diagnostics — anonymous screen-transition timing (20% sample) used to detect slow screens and fix them. Sent to Sentry along with crash reports.

Third-Party Services

The App uses exactly two third-party services, both bound by contractual data-protection agreements:

  • RevenueCat (privacy policy) — subscription management. Receives the anonymous device identifier and purchase state described above.
  • Sentry (privacy policy) — crash and performance diagnostics. Receives the anonymous crash / diagnostic data described above.

All data transmitted to these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Neither service receives content your child produces, coloring pictures, session history, badges, or gameplay data.

Data Deletion Requests

Even though the data described above is anonymous, we honor deletion requests. To have your data removed, email colorbymath@highergroundcap.com from any address you like and tell us "please delete my data associated with Color by Math." We will remove the associated records from both RevenueCat and Sentry within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you don't want to wait, uninstalling the App removes all on-device data immediately (game progress, badges, saved pictures). Anonymous data already sent to Sentry is automatically deleted within 90 days regardless of whether you request removal. RevenueCat data is retained for as long as the App is installed on your device or you have an active subscription; deletion on request removes it earlier.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is designed for children ages 6 and up and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and equivalent children's privacy laws worldwide.

  • No personal information is collected from children or adults
  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No behavioral analytics or usage telemetry beyond what's described above (anonymous crash / performance diagnostics)
  • No advertising of any kind — no banners, video ads, or advertising SDKs
  • In-app purchases are gated behind a parental gate that requires solving a math problem designed to be hard for young children
  • The only third-party SDKs that transmit data are RevenueCat and Sentry, described above

Offline Gameplay

All gameplay runs 100% offline. Coloring pages, audio clips, translations, math problems, badges, and the child's saved progress are entirely on-device. The App makes no network requests during play. The only network activity that occurs is:

  • A one-time RevenueCat sync at app launch if your device is online (to check subscription state)
  • Crash upload if the App actually crashes
  • Google Play / App Store purchase flow if you tap "Buy Premium"

Parental Controls

Features that leave the App context (saving art to the device photo library, sharing art, accessing settings, opening the Parent Dashboard, purchasing Premium) are protected by a parental gate that requires solving a math problem designed to be hard for young children. After three wrong answers in a row, the gate enters a cooldown period to discourage guessing.

Photo Library Access

If a parent chooses to save a completed coloring to the device photo library, the App will request permission to access the photo library. This permission is triggered only by explicit parental action behind the parental gate. No photos are uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Data Storage

The App stores game progress (completed pages, star ratings, adaptive Smart Mode level, earned badges, session history, settings preferences, selected language) locally on the device using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the App is uninstalled. Parents can reset this data at any time from the Parent Dashboard, which also keeps a one-time backup for recovery.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and include a notice in the App's update notes.
